localtransit.app

This website is designed to show a visualization of transit service, patterns, and routes automatically generated from publicly-available GTFS data.

While the visualizations here are cool to look at, this website is not intended to be used as a trip planner, please consult official agency information for trip planning.

Supported Agencies

Click on any agency below to see that agency's stops on a map colored by frequency.
Selecting any headers will map all agencies included under the respective header.
